## Limits

The 480px-height accessibility fallback allows scrolling when a keyboard, zoom or short landscape window needs it. Factory host, phones, model accuracy and native packages are separate acceptance work. A finished site layout does not establish those capabilities. Private ledger authentication is covered by existing tests; no claim of a fresh live owner sign-in.
